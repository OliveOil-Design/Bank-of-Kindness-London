About the project

The Bank of Kindness, London

A small experiment in everyday kindness — strangers leaving something warm for the next stranger, nothing more.

Free to fork for your own city. The code is open source.

The idea for this project came from a Good Eye session, and was developed by Oliver Lloyd.
